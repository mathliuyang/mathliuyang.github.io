P0125	style=	num=:	“In recent years, deep learning has been used not only for differential equations [19-23] but also increasingly for integral equations. PINN-based methods have been developed for multidimensional Fredholm integral equations [24,25], volume integral equations [26], boundary integral equations [27] and other multidimensional integral equations with continuous kernels [28]. For integral equations with discontinuous coefficients, domain-decomposed XPINNs are often used to accommodate non-smooth solutions across interfaces [29]. DeepONet and neural-operator methods are suitable for solving a class of parameterized problems [21,30-32], thereby enhancing the generalization capability of deep learning based numerical computation. Most of these studies concern non-singular integral equations and evaluate the integral terms by numerical integration, which introduces additional discretization error. The boundary integral neural network (BINN) is an important exception: it converts a PDE into a boundary integral equation using a Green’s function and evaluates weakly singular and Cauchy-type singular integrals by regularization techniques. Its applicability, however, depends on the existence of the BIE formulation and the fundamental solution. In many non-linear problems, the unknowns in the interior region cannot be completely eliminated. Moreover, the fundamental solution may be hard to obtain or even not exist [27]. Therefore, it is necessary to explore new deep learning methods for solving singular integral equations.”
